How the projects are judged

Not by how futuristic the interface sounds—by whether the system explains itself and survives inspection.

Product test

  • Purpose understood in seconds.
  • Useful interaction beyond a screenshot.
  • Clear next action and direct path to evidence.

Engineering test

  • Assumptions and limitations are explicit.
  • Outputs and reasoning can be inspected.
  • Simulated vs real data is clearly disclosed.
