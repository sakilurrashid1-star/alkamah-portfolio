You are the on-call security engineer. A suspicious workstation is beaconing to an unfamiliar host while privileged activity appears in the same window. You have 10 minutes to triage, contain, preserve evidence and communicate a defensible decision.

Mission brief

Incident: FIN-WS-042 shows unusual outbound traffic at 02:13 UTC. Five minutes later, a privileged account authenticates from the same subnet.

Constraints: You cannot shut down the entire subnet. Evidence must remain useful for later investigation. Assume the attacker may still be active.

Objective: Make the highest-value decision at each stage. There may be more than one reasonable action—but some choices destroy evidence, increase blast radius, or delay containment.
